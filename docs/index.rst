Welcome to pygcc's documentation!
===================================

**pygcc** (/paɪˈdʒiːsiːsiː/) is a python-based program for thermodynamic calculations and producing the Geochemist's Workbench (GWB), EQ3/6, TOUGHREACT, and PFLOTRAN thermodynamic database from ambient to deep Earth temperature and pressure conditions

Check out the :doc:`installation` section for further information, including
how to :ref:`installation` the project.
